## Failure behavior

Before a terminal commit, the approved unsaved-result state retains the result in the open page and offers exact retry; Continue is disabled. If an earned phase already committed, this newly adopted fixed owner can reconstruct the command after reload. Before that phase commit, closing may lose the most recent uncommitted actions.

After a terminal transaction commits, loss of its acknowledgment or a failed legacy mirror does not permit another completion effect. The terminal record and its dependencies determine the result. A canonical readback is required before Continue.

Missing, unsupported or mismatching records block recovery. A missing adoption record alone does not prove an empty store. Old localStorage writes, old RAM-overlay retries and native-key deletion never become authoritative input for this owner.

IndexedDB transaction completion with a strict durability hint is the tested boundary. Graceful reload tests do not prove survival of process crashes, power loss, browser eviction, origin clearing or device loss. No local record authenticates gameplay or replaces server verification.
