RULES & REWARDS

📜
MISSION PROTOCOLHow Moon Points, lives and future rewards work.
01
9 LIVES

Complete LIFE #1 through LIFE #9 — one new chapter per day: after you finish a chapter, the next one opens at 00:00 UTC (an invited friend who completes LIFE #1 can open one chapter early). You have 9 global lives: every failed board, flight or chapter finale costs 1 life (training and the first LIFE #1 tutorial are free). At 0 lives missions pause until a life restores — each spent life restores after 12 hours.

02
MOON POINTS ⭐

Earn Moon Points from eligible story missions, verified Daily Missions, verified referral activity and approved creator videos while your 9 LIVES journey is active.

03
DAILY & COMMUNITY

Opening a link never earns points. Telegram channel: +5 ⭐ once, after the server confirms your membership through the Telegram Bot API. X and TikTok: post, reply or comment with your personal code (MKTY-XXXX) and paste the link; the team reviews it — +5 ⭐ once per platform for following and +5 ⭐ per UTC day per platform for a post or comment. YouTube: points only for code words from videos. Repeated, fake or unverified actions do not create rewards.

🎬
CREATOR REWARDS

Your own video about MOONKATTY with #MOONKATTY can earn Moon Points after manual review: up to one submission per week, each video once, view tiers 1,000 and 10,000 granted once each. Creator points count toward your final balance like other Moon Points. Bought views, reuploads and other people’s content are rejected.

04
REFERRALS

Invite friends with your personal link. You and your friend each receive +200 Moon Points only after your friend completes LIFE #1, verified by the server. Self-referrals do not count. Inviter rewards are limited to 10 verified events per UTC day and 100 in total.

05
FINAL BALANCE 🔒

After successful completion of LIFE #9, Moon Point earning ends. Your accumulated Moon Points are totaled and locked as your FINAL MOON POINTS BALANCE.

06
GAME POINTS

Moon Points are in-game points for standings and game rewards. They have no monetary value and do not guarantee prizes or payments.

Terms of use · Privacy policy

07
FAIR PLAY

Duplicate activity, self-referrals, automation, manipulation or other abuse may be excluded from rewards. Reward limits and verification parameters may be adjusted to protect the game economy.

08
DAILY SIGNALS 📡

Once per UTC day: login streak +3 to +25 ⭐ (day 7 cap; one shield per week saves one missed day), Moon Signal cipher +15 ⭐ with 5 attempts, and YouTube code words up to +50 ⭐ each, once per code. All are verified by the server through Telegram.
